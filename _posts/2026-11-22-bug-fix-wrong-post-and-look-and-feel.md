The line under my name should read Quality & Delivery Lead. I want that wording in that spot, and I don't want the keyword line there instead: QA Lead, Test Automation, and Agile Delivery. That keyword line is the wrong line under the name, and that's only a preference about where those phrases sit.

The role line and the writing line are not the same line, and I want them kept apart. The first should sit under my name. The second should stay the description of this blog, written as its own sentence, and I don't want it folded into the headline or treated as a second title. Writing about software quality and engineering.

The headline names the role. The writing stays about the work.
