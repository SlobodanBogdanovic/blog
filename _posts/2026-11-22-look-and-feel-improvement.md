I'm changing the line under my name to Quality Engineering | Agile Delivery. The name stays Slobodan Bogdanović, and I'm keeping the sentence "Writing about software quality and engineering."

A role label is a narrower description than the subjects I've already been writing about.

## A frame for reading

I want a Linux-desktop chrome, including a home page that reads like a short shell session, so a long post stays comfortable to read. I'm not claiming this theme is already on the site.

I want body text in Inter, and JetBrains Mono only for the chrome and for code, at 18px with a line height of 1.7 and a measure of about 68ch. Light and dark follow the system, on the Catppuccin palette I named, Mocha in the dark and Latte in the light. Text below WCAG AA (4.5:1) has to be adjusted, 4.5:1 is the threshold I named, and this post isn't that check.

## What stays

The page stays a name, that headline, links to GitHub and LinkedIn only, then the list of posts. There's no email, and there are no new sections. I'm not rewriting published posts, and I'm not changing their URLs.

The shell chrome is a frame. The post is the part a reader should be able to finish.
