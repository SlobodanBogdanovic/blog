Over the years I have collected a set of books that keep coming back in my work: planning and delivering software, building high-performing teams, and growing from engineer to leader. Here is the list, divided into groups so it is easier to pick what to read next.

## Agile and Delivery

The craft of planning, estimating, specifying, testing, and managing risk in software projects.

- **Agile Estimating and Planning** — Mike Cohn
- **Agile Software Requirements** — Dean Leffingwell
- **Agile Testing** — Lisa Crispin & Janet Gregory
- **Waltzing with Bears** — Tom DeMarco & Timothy Lister

## Product, Flow and Organizational Performance

How organizations deliver value at scale, and what makes teams and teams of teams perform.

- **Project to Product** — Mik Kersten
- **Accelerate** — Nicole Forsgren, Jez Humble & Gene Kim
- **Team of Teams** — Gen. Stanley McChrystal

## Getting Things Done and Communication

Moving work forward, having the hard conversations, and coaching people instead of solving everything for them.

- **Making Things Happen** — Scott Berkun
- **Crucial Conversations** — Patterson, Grenny, McMillan & Switzler
- **The Coaching Habit** — Michael Bungay Stanier

## Engineering Leadership Path

A suggested reading order, from technical lead through management to the executive level.

1. **Leveling Up as a Technical Lead** — Anemari Fiser
2. **Become an Effective Software Engineering Manager** — James Stanier
3. **The Engineering Executive's Primer** — Will Larson
4. **Leading Effective Engineering Teams** — Addy Osmani
5. **The Manager's Path** — Camille Fournier
6. **The Staff Engineer's Path** — Tanya Reilly
7. **The Software Engineer's Guidebook** — Gergely Orosz
